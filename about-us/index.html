A Canadian team built for independent hotel owners

We're RateHarbor, a Canadian company that provides outsourced hotel revenue management and OTA management to hotel owners.

  • Working directly with you

    We work for hotel owners and operators, not travellers, and you agree every engagement directly with our team.

  • Independent hotels worldwide

    We work with independent hotels and small hotel groups in many countries, including the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and Australia.

  • Our specialist team

    There are 12 of us, and we work across revenue management, OTA management and owner reporting.

Full transparency on our credentials and your accounts

We work through the user access you give us in your own accounts. We don't claim official partner status with Booking.com, Expedia or any other platform. Our revenue specialists hold industry-recognised revenue management certifications. Your proposal lists every certification each specialist holds.

If you ask, we'll send you copies of the supporting documents before you sign.

Your property, owned by one dedicated specialist

  • Hands-on hotel experience

    Your dedicated specialist has at least 5 years of hands-on hotel revenue management and OTA experience with independent and boutique properties.

  • A defined property limit

    As part of our service for hotel owners, each specialist looks after no more than 10 properties.

  • Account ownership and coverage

    Under our working process, one specialist owns your account from start to finish and handles the daily decisions. You get pricing decisions every day, a weekly or monthly strategy call and cover from the wider team on all 365 days of the year.

  • Evidence before you sign

    If you ask before you sign, we'll share the case studies closest to your property's type and size. They show RevPAR, occupancy and ADR before and after we started. We'll also share an anonymised sample monthly performance report that shows how we track these figures.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you also work for hotels that compete with mine in the same town?

Yes, we can work with more than one property in the same town, but your rates, booking data and strategy stay private to you. If this is a concern, raise it in your consultation and we'll set out how we keep competing properties separate in your proposal.

Do you take commissions from the OTAs or software vendors you recommend?

No. We don't accept referral commissions from software vendors or OTAs, so our channel recommendations are based only on what fits your property. You pay us a flat monthly retainer, and we take no commission on your room revenue. You pay the OTA commissions on your bookings directly to each platform.

Are you independent, or part of a larger group or hotel brand?

We're an independent Canadian business-to-business company. We aren't part of any hotel group, hotel brand, OTA or software vendor. Our team of 12 works only for the hotel owners and operators who hire us. We don't claim official partner status with Booking.com, Expedia or any other platform. We work through the user access you give us in your own accounts.

Can I see results for properties like mine before I sign?

Yes. If you ask, we'll share full case studies before you sign. We pick the cases closest to your property and show RevPAR, occupancy and ADR before and after we started. We'll also send an anonymised sample monthly performance report so you can see how we track those figures.
