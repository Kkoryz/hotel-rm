What to Send for an Exact Proposal

To get an exact proposal, send us your property details along with at least 12 months of booking history, or 24 months if you have it.

For that period, your revenue management proposal needs your monthly occupancy, ADR and RevPAR, plus your channel mix across OTAs and direct bookings. Please also include your room count, room types, current rate plans, channel manager and the 3 to 5 competitors you watch.

That lets us set the right room-count tier and scope.

How the Monthly Retainer Is Billed and What It Covers

Under our payment terms, prices are in USD excluding sales tax; Canadian clients add GST/HST. We invoice managed plans in advance on the 1st, due within 14 days.

  • Payment methods and timing

    You can pay by bank transfer or a major credit card. One-time services are paid in full at booking, and every proposal and contract shows our full legal name and registered business address.

  • Monthly flexibility

    You can stop any managed plan at the end of any month, and there's no long-term lock-in.

  • Flat retainer

    We don't charge a revenue commission, a percentage or a performance bonus, so your monthly fee stays the same in slow months.

  • Included activity

    Your plan has no cap on daily rate changes, reports or strategy calls.

  • Separate project fees

    Work outside your plan's scope, such as PriceLabs sessions or OTA listing optimisation, has a fixed one-time fee. We agree that fee with you in writing before the work starts.

  • Additional team training

    Extra remote team training is quoted separately, on top of your retainer.

  • Third-party costs

    Setup and retainer fees don't include OTA commissions, software subscriptions or ad and metasearch spend. You pay those providers directly.

Bundle Several Services With Priority Turnaround

If you need several one-time services now, request them together.

We'll bundle them, put them in the right order and give them priority turnaround. Coordinating work that depends on other work, such as pricing-tool setup and OTA listing optimisation, avoids rework and conflicting settings. Bundling only pays off when you need those services now.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I send if my property is new and has no recent performance figures?

Send us your room count, room types, opening date, budget and any bookings you already have. If you have no booking history, we build your proposal from competitor-set rates and market demand data, and we price from those sources and your budget. As reservations come in, we switch to your own booking data. Pricing in the first months is less precise and needs closer review. Once a property has booking history, its pricing is more precise because it's based on its own data.

Can I ask for one proposal covering several properties?

Yes. Just list each property's type, room count and location in a single request. We work with hotels, motels, B&Bs, serviced apartments and short-term rentals. Each property is priced in its own room-count tier, and we deliver the work in batches. You get one document that sets out the scope, fees and start order for each property.

Can I see a sample proposal before I send my details?

Yes. Before you share your details, we can email you an anonymised sample proposal. If you ask, we also send an anonymised monthly performance report, full case studies for you to review before you sign.

Will you visit the property before you write the proposal?

No. We work only remotely, inside your hotel's own systems and online accounts, and we don't have on-site staff at any property.
