RateHarbor Blog

Guides for independent hotel owners and Airbnb hosts on room pricing, OTA listings and rate parity.

  • How much do OTAs charge hotels?

    17 November 2026

    OTAs typically charge hotels **15%–30% or more** of the applicable booking value, according to Cloudbeds’ A Guide to OTA Commission Rates. How much your hotel keeps depends on the commission base in your agreement, hotel-funded discounts, optional program charges and payment costs.

    Read more →

  • Hotel rate parity issues: verify undercuts and fix the cause

    17 November 2026

    Hotel rate parity issues arise when channels show different prices for equivalent stays. Verify the offer through checkout, separate the guest discount from any change in hotel payout, and trace the source before choosing a fix.

    Read more →

  • What does a revenue manager do for a hotel?

    17 November 2026

    A hotel revenue manager forecasts demand, sets room rates and availability, and manages booking channels to improve financial performance. They coordinate with hotel teams and review results so the owner can understand what changed, why it changed and whether it helped. Cvent's hotel revenue manager guide explains these responsibilities.

Who the RateHarbor Blog Is For

The blog covers room pricing, OTA listings, rate parity and performance reporting for owners of independent hotels, small hotel groups and other lodging properties, drawing on our remote revenue management and OTA management work.
