What You'll Find on the RateHarbor Blog

Our blog covers room pricing, OTA listings, rate parity and performance reporting for owners of independent hotels, small hotel groups and other lodging properties.

We write it from our remote revenue management and OTA management work. We work with independent hotels and small hotel groups in many countries, including the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and Australia. You can take these services and our related online services separately or together.

We don't run your hotel operations. We work only remotely. We use your hotel's own systems and online accounts, and we have no staff on site at any property.
