About RateHarbor

We handle outsourced hotel revenue management and OTA management, so you can bring us in for those services while your hotel operations stay with you.

Our 12-person Canadian team works with independent hotels and small hotel groups in countries including the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and Australia. You can take our revenue management, OTA management and related online services separately or together.

Revenue management covers pricing and rate strategy, demand forecasting, competitive set monitoring and regular performance reports for owners.

Context: OTA Listings, Rates and Parity

Our OTA management service looks after your property's listings and rates on Booking.com, Expedia and other online travel agencies, including your content, rate parity and channel manager settings.

Results You Can Check Before You Sign

  • Full case studies

    Just ask, and we'll share full case studies before you sign. They show before-and-after RevPAR, occupancy and ADR, and we pick the cases closest to your property's type and size.

  • Relevant comparisons

    Ask for cases close to your property's type, size and market. Before-and-after figures are only a useful comparison when the property and its demand resemble yours.

  • Monthly measurement

    We'll also send you a sample monthly report. Read it alongside the cases to check how we track those figures each month. Both are anonymised to protect other owners.

  • Niche property matches

    If your property is unusual or niche, our closest available comparison may differ in location or seasonality, so take that difference into account when you assess the results.

  • Remote working arrangements

    Under our remote working process, we work through your hotel's own systems and online accounts. We don't place on-site staff at any property.

  • Dynamic pricing results

    With our Dynamic Pricing Strategy service, 8 of the 11 hotels we took on in 2025 saw measurable results within 60 to 90 days, with no results guaranteed.

Client results

Digital Marketing

For a 34-room independent motel in Ontario, we rebuilt its OTA listings, fixed rate parity and launched direct-booking search campaigns. From March 2024 to February 2025 against the previous 12 months, its share of room nights booked direct rose from 9% to 21%, and its Booking.com content score (the extranet's own listing score) went from 62% to 100%.

Boutique Hotel Consulting

For a 42-room independent boutique hotel in Cornwall, we realigned pricing, OTA presence and direct booking with the owner's new upscale positioning. In April–September 2025 against the same months of 2024, its RevPAR index (its RevPAR ÷ its competitive set's) rose from 0.93 to 1.07, and its share of room nights booked direct went from 14% to 26%.

Commercial Strategy

For a three-hotel independent city-centre group in Queensland, we combined forecasting, pricing and channel strategy across the portfolio and sent the owners monthly reports. In 2025 against 2024, the three hotels' combined RevPAR index (their RevPAR ÷ their competitive sets') rose from 0.97 to 1.09, and their share of room nights booked through OTAs fell from 68% to 55%.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you have a case for a property my size in a small or rural market?

We work with lodging properties of any size in small towns and rural areas as well as cities, with no minimum room count. Ask us before you sign and we'll share the case studies closest to your property's type, size and market.

How long after you started did the results show?

Most hotels we price for see measurable results within 60 to 90 days, judged on RevPAR against the same weeks a year earlier. We agree measurable targets with you at the start and track them in your monthly performance report, but we don't guarantee specific revenue, occupancy or ADR results.

Can I see how you report results before I sign?

Yes. Before you sign, we send you an anonymised sample monthly performance report alongside our case studies, so you can see how we track RevPAR, occupancy and ADR.
