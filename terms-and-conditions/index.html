Who We Are

We provide outsourced hotel revenue management and OTA management for hotel owners.

Every proposal and contract we send includes our full legal name and registered business address. We carry professional liability and cyber liability insurance that covers the remote work we do in your systems and accounts. If you ask, we'll send you a certificate of insurance before you sign.

It shows the cover we hold.

How We Work With Hotel Owners

We're a Canadian business-to-business service company.

That means we agree every engagement directly with you as a hotel owner or operator, never with travellers. You can take our services and related online services on their own or together, and we don't run hotel operations.

With our remote working process, we work only in your hotel's own systems and online accounts. We have no on-site staff at any property.

Billing, Cancellation and Ownership of Your Work

We run managed plans month to month, with no long-term lock-in.

  • Managed plan billing

    We invoice managed plans as a monthly retainer, in advance, on the 1st of each month. Payment is due within 14 days.

  • Currency and taxes

    Our prices are in USD and don't include sales tax. If you're a Canadian client, we charge the applicable GST/HST.

  • Payments and booking

    You can pay by bank transfer or major credit card. You pay for one-time services in full when you book them.

  • Stopping a plan

    You can stop any managed plan at the end of any month if you tell us in writing before that month ends. If a month has already started, you pay for it in full.

  • Cancellation and onboarding

    There's no long-term lock-in and no cancellation fee. If you cancel during onboarding, you pay only for the onboarding work we've already done.

  • Ownership of your work

    You own every report, pricing framework, budget, piece of listing content and account we build for you.

  • Your OTA setup

    When we stop working for you, your OTA accounts, channel mappings and settings stay as they are.

  • Handover to your team

    Within 10 business days after our engagement ends, we give your team the current rate settings, documentation and files. We also walk your staff through them on a handover video call.
