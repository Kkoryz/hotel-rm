Outsourced Revenue and OTA Support for Hotels

We provide outsourced hotel revenue and OTA management for owners of independent hotels and small hotel groups in many countries, including the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and Australia. Our revenue management service covers your pricing and rate strategy, demand forecasting and competitive set monitoring.

It also includes regular performance reports for you.

Services Built Around Your Property's Revenue Goals

Hotel Digital Marketing

Our hotel digital marketing brings you more direct bookings. We use a website that converts, Google Ads and metasearch run in accounts you own, and email campaigns that we track.

Boutique Hotel Consulting

With our boutique hotel consulting, we sharpen your positioning and move your guests from OTAs to direct bookings. Then we hand the work over to your team.

Commercial Strategy

We build your hotel commercial strategy on market analysis and input from your sales team. We also choose and set up your technology, and you get flexible ways to work with us.

OTA Optimization

Our OTA optimization improves your OTA ranking and brings in more bookings. We rebuild your Expedia and Agoda listings, fix rate parity and review your placement programmes.

Dynamic Pricing Strategy

We build your hotel dynamic pricing strategy live in your pricing tool. It covers every room type and benchmarks you against your competitive set, and we keep it current after launch.

Hotel Distribution

With our hotel distribution support, we allocate your rooms based on booking data and commission costs. We also handle your channel mix, wholesale, new listings and rate parity.

Revenue Management Audit

Our hotel revenue management audit shows you where pricing, forecasting and distribution are losing you revenue and what each fix is worth. It also sets out your priorities for action.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which of these services should I start with if my bookings are down?

Start with a revenue audit. It tells you whether pricing, OTA visibility or channel mix is behind the drop in bookings. For a single property, we deliver the audit within 10 business days of getting your data and access. If your listings rank poorly, our one-time Booking.com listing optimisation takes 2 to 3 business days. If the audit finds several causes, we bundle it with managed pricing or OTA management and give you priority turnaround.

Does one person handle all the services for my property, or is there a different specialist for each?

One dedicated specialist owns your account from start to finish, with our wider team behind them. Your specialist is part of our 12-person team and has at least 5 years of hands-on hotel revenue management and OTA experience with independent and boutique properties. No specialist looks after more than 10 properties, and our wider team gives you coverage all 365 days of the year.

Is the revenue management audit a one-off, or does it lead into ongoing work?

You can buy a revenue management audit as a one-off, and you have no obligation to continue. You can also use it as the starting point for ongoing work. Revenue audits and strategy roadmaps have a fixed scope and a fixed fee. We quote the fee after your free 30-minute consultation. For a single property, we deliver the audit report within 10 business days of getting your data and access. For hotel groups, every batch of up to 5 group properties adds 10 business days. If you go on to ongoing work, we start with discovery and an audit and then agree a strategy with you. After that come day-to-day management and regular reviews of your results. Every consulting engagement finishes with remote training and a handover to your own staff.
