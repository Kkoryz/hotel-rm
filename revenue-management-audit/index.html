Three Ways to Audit Your Hotel's Commercial Performance

You can choose a focused Revenue Management Audit, a wider Profitability Audit, or both together for a complete view of your hotel's commercial performance.

We independently assess your strategy, systems, processes and results, then turn what we find into prioritised actions. Choose the Revenue Management Audit if you want to lift RevPAR, make your forecasts more reliable, find revenue leakage or test whether your current approach is achieving everything it can.

Compare Audit Formats, Timelines and Pricing

We provide outsourced hotel revenue management and OTA management for hotel owners. Here's how the audit options compare.

  • Revenue Management Audit

    What it means for you
    It focuses on pricing, forecasting and distribution, and it involves your revenue and front office leads.

  • Profitability Audit

    What it means for you
    It tests why revenue growth isn't raising GOP, where your margins leak and whether your profit lasts. It also needs your commission and distribution cost statements, your commercial contracts, and input from your finance lead and your owner or asset manager.

  • Combined audit

    What it means for you
    It covers both scopes in full depth within the same delivery window. It suits owners, investors, management companies and asset managers who judge performance on profit. Preparing all the data it needs adds about one week on your side.

  • Property coverage

    What it means for you
    We audit individual properties, and we audit small groups in batches. That covers hotels, motels, boutique hotels, resorts, B&Bs, serviced apartments and short-term rentals, and there's no minimum room count.

  • Pricing factors

    What it means for you
    Your price depends on the format you choose, how many properties you have, your room counts, and any add-ons such as extra training days or implementation support.

What the Revenue Management Audit Reviews

Competitive set

Before we benchmark anything, we agree 5 to 8 competing properties with you that match your location, property type, size and price level.

Benchmarking without subscriptions

If you don't have paid market-share data, we use competitor rates, booking pace, pickup, OTA market data and pricing tools instead, and we mark every comparison that needs paid data to confirm it.

Demand and positioning

We look at your market position and the market-share evidence that's available, along with your demand drivers, seasonal swings and booking patterns.

Pricing architecture

We assess your pricing logic and room-type differentials to establish how your rate structure supports your market positioning.

Forecasts and budgets

We assess how accurate your forecasts are and how you build your budgets, so we can check the foundations of your revenue expectations.

Inventory and yielding

We review your availability controls, restrictions, overbooking and inventory, and we measure how quickly your pricing and yielding respond to demand and booking pace.

Mix and distribution

We evaluate your segmentation, business mix, channel contribution, rate parity, commissions, direct booking performance and overall distribution effectiveness.

Groups and displacement

If you take group or contracted business, we review your quotations, your displacement analysis and how you balance that business against transient demand.

Systems and reporting

We check how your PMS, CRS, RMS, channel manager and business-intelligence tools are set up and used, including your data quality and reporting accuracy.

Revenue governance

We look at your meeting frequency, decision rights, responsibilities and standard operating procedures, then check whether the revenue actions you agree are actually carried through.

Record reconciliation

Before we draw any conclusions, we reconcile your PMS, channel manager, OTA extranet and financial records, and we flag every figure we can't verify.

Missing data limits

When evidence is missing, it limits what we can conclude about forecast accuracy, net channel contribution and year-on-year trends. Your pricing, rate structures, restrictions and system setup stay fully reviewable.

PriceLabs setup

If you already have a PriceLabs setup, our Dynamic Pricing Strategy service audits it for leakage and missed growth opportunities, and that takes 1 to 2 business days.

Distribution implementation

Our Hotel Distribution service allocates your inventory across OTAs, GDS and direct channels based on booking trends and commissions, and it controls your rates and availability to maintain parity and reduce overbooking risk.

Profitability Audit: Margins, Acquisition Costs and Net Contribution

  • Commercial profitability

    We work out what it costs you to win each booking and what each channel, segment and revenue source contributes net. We then check those figures against the rooms revenue lines in your P&L.

  • Direct acquisition costs

    OTA commissions, payment fees, brand and loyalty fees, and paid campaign spend are charged directly to the channel or segment that incurs them.

  • Shared cost allocation

    Some marketing, distribution and sales costs can't be traced to one channel. We split those by share of room-nights or room revenue, using the method we agree with you at scoping.

  • Transparent contribution calculations

    We show allocated costs separately, next to gross revenue and net contribution, so you can test a different split yourself.

  • Commercial contract terms

    We review your commercial contracts together with your marketing spend, distribution costs and sales costs to see how they affect your margins.

  • Reporting and alignment

    We look at how much visibility your P&L gives you, how good your ownership reporting is and how well aligned your commercial teams are.

  • Growth business cases

    We check that each revenue-growth initiative has a clear business case that's measured against profit, not just revenue.

  • Operating cost boundary

    We don't run hotel operations, and we don't audit payroll, utilities, food costs or staffing. General operating costs are left out of the channel calculations, but we flag GOP constraints.

From Scoping to Recommendations in Four Steps

  1. 01

    Agree the scope

    We confirm your priorities and challenges with you and settle whether the audit covers revenue management, profitability or both.

  2. 02

    Review the evidence

    Our experts review and analyse your performance data, reports, systems, processes, documentation and commercial strategies.

  3. 03

    Validate with stakeholders

    We interview your stakeholders so we understand the context. In those interviews we challenge assumptions and test the opportunities we've found before we present them.

  4. 04

    Present the recommendations

    We walk you through our findings and prioritised recommendations, along with a roadmap that shows your team what to tackle first.

Data, Access and Time Needed From Your Team

Send us your property details and at least 12 months of booking history.

If you have 24 months, send all 24. Break down reservations, room revenue and rates by date, room type, channel and rate plan. We also ask for your P&L, your current budget and forecast, and your competitive-set reports.

If your property is newly opened and has no booking history, we work from competitive-set rates, market demand data and your budget instead.

Give each of our specialists a named login to your PMS, channel manager, RMS and OTA extranets, set at the lowest permission level the task needs.

Your team will need to give us a 60-minute kickoff call plus remote video interviews of about 45 minutes each with up to 4 stakeholders. That comes to about 4 to 6 hours in total.

Reports, Action Plan and Roadmap You Receive

We tailor what we deliver to the audit you choose, your priorities and the quality of the data you have.

Executive Audit Report

You get an assessment of your current performance, strategies, systems and processes that points out the main opportunities for improvement.

80/20 Action Plan

We rank every recommendation by expected impact, urgency and implementation effort. That gives your team a clear order of priorities.

Opportunity estimates

When your data supports it, we estimate your revenue or profit gains. Each estimate states the rate, occupancy and channel-mix assumptions behind it, plus identifiable commissions, software fees and staff time. These gains aren't guaranteed.

90-Day Implementation Roadmap

We sort actions into immediate, short-term and medium-term priorities. Each one has a baseline KPI, a measurable success criterion and a review date.

Performance checks

The KPIs cover RevPAR, ADR, occupancy, forecast accuracy and net channel contribution. We compare them against last year, your budget and your competitive set, so you can tell real changes apart from seasonality and market shifts.

Presentation and supporting analysis

We present our findings and recommendations to your ownership, your leadership and the teams involved. Performance analysis, benchmarks, observations and decision frameworks back them up.

Individual property findings

In a multi-property audit, each hotel gets its own findings. We benchmark them against that hotel's competitive set and its results for the same period last year.

Consolidated group comparison

We group your properties by market, type and size so we don't compare unlike hotels. Then we rank opportunities across your portfolio by expected impact.

Fixed Fee, Clear Timelines and Handover to Your Team

Our fixed-scope consulting work takes you from an agreed audit brief, through our recommendations, to a handover once your team is trained.

You don't have to sign up for an ongoing engagement. For a single property, we deliver the audit report within 10 business days of receiving your data and access. Each additional batch of up to 5 group properties adds 10 business days.

If you want ongoing management, our process starts with discovery. That covers your pricing, channel mix, OTA positioning, past performance and competitors. From there we agree goals, time and cost estimates and a strategy with you, then move on to daily revenue management and regular reviews.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this audit the same as the revenue audit you do at the start of a managed engagement, or is it a separate, deeper paid engagement?

It's a separate paid engagement with a fixed scope. You get a written report and roadmap. We quote a fixed fee after a free 30-minute consultation, and you don't have to continue with us afterwards. The discovery audit in a managed plan is different. It reviews your pricing, channel mix, OTA positioning and past performance so we can set up daily management. If you later choose a monthly plan, this audit becomes that discovery step. If your hotel has its own revenue team, our consulting audit uses the same format, and you can extend it to cover profitability.

Can you complete the audit using read-only access or data exports without permission to change our live rates and system settings?

Yes. We work from read-only logins or exports from your PMS, RMS, channel manager and OTA extranets, and we have no permission to change your live rates or settings. Each of our specialists gets their own named login with the lowest permissions the work needs. We never use a shared password. We stop using your systems on the last day and confirm this in writing within 5 business days. You then remove our logins.

How many weeks does a Revenue Management Audit, a Profitability Audit or a combined audit take from start to final presentation?

For a single property, each format takes 3 to 4 weeks from scoping to the management presentation. We deliver the written report within 10 business days of receiving your data and access. Each additional batch of up to 5 group properties adds 10 business days. Data collection and stakeholder interviews take the longest, and data collection moves as fast as you send us your exports and logins. We schedule the interviews within the first week of the review.

After you deliver the roadmap, can you help us put it into action or check our progress later?

Yes. We offer implementation support with a fixed scope, which we quote separately, and video training your team can work through at its own pace. You can book a follow-up KPI review. You can also move to monthly management, and the audit then serves as the discovery step. Every consulting engagement ends with remote training and a handover to your staff.

Do we receive editable analysis files and calculation assumptions that our own team can inspect and update, or only the finished report and presentation?

You get both. Along with the finished report and presentation, you receive editable supporting spreadsheets. They record every calculation assumption and data source, so your team can check and update the figures. You own all the reports and files we create. We hand over the documentation and files within 10 business days after the engagement ends.

Can you audit our revenue management when a brand or external operator controls pricing, contracts or system access, and what would remain outside your review?

Yes. We hold the pricing, forecasting, distribution and reporting your provider controls to the same standards we use for our own work. We work from the reports, exports and read-only access the provider gives us, and we mark which areas they control. Our conclusions leave out brand rate programmes we can't see, central reservation rules, corporate or wholesale contracts that haven't been disclosed to us, and the provider's internal decisions. We list these exclusions so you can raise them with the provider. The audit is a standalone job, and you aren't committed to any further work. We don't accept referral fees from OTAs or software vendors, and we recommend only what suits your property. We also point out the arrangements that should stay as they are.
