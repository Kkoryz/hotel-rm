Booking.com listings rebuilt to rank higher and convert more

We work to bring your Booking.com content score to 100%, keeping your existing reviews intact.

As part of our OTA optimization, we update your title, description, photos, policies and amenity tags, refine your room content, choose your main photo and set your gallery order. We also set up your rate plans, Genius and promotions, with policies built around conversion.

We make every change live in your extranet while we're with you on Zoom, so you watch each adjustment happen. Listings we optimize usually rise in Booking.com search rankings within 2 to 4 weeks.

An outsourced OTA and revenue team for independent hotels

  • Listing management

    We run your property's listings, including their content, on Booking.com, Expedia and other online travel agencies.

  • Channel controls

    Our OTA management service also covers your rates, rate parity and channel manager settings.

  • Your outsourced team

    Our 12-person team provides outsourced revenue management, OTA management and owner reporting for independent hotels and small hotel groups internationally.

  • Remote working

    With our remote process, all our work stays inside your hotel's own systems and online accounts, and we send no staff on site.

Expedia Group optimization and full listing rebuilds

Expedia Group coverage

We optimize your listings on Expedia, Hotels.com, Orbitz and Travelocity, work towards a 100% content score and restructure your rooms and rate plans.

Existing account rebuilds

If your listing has been neglected, we rebuild it from scratch inside your existing property account, so you keep your property ID, guest reviews and existing reservations.

Structure before promotion

We fix your content, rooms and rate structure first and only then add promotions, member deals or Accelerator, so placement work builds on the rebuilt listing.

Agoda YCS optimization and Preferred Partner eligibility

In Agoda's YCS, we optimize your property and room content, your photos and your rate channels. We plan your secret deals, mobile rates and promotions as one structure, and we check whether your property is eligible for Preferred Partner before we recommend the programme.

If your Agoda listing has been neglected, we rebuild it completely.

Google Hotel Ads, free booking links and metasearch

Live metasearch feeds

We connect your rates and availability straight to Google free booking links, Google Hotel Ads, trivago, TripAdvisor and Kayak.

Your Google presence

Along with connecting your live rates and availability, our metasearch work covers your Google Business Profile and your reviews.

Account and payment ownership

Your ad accounts stay in your property's name. You pay media spend directly to each platform with your own payment method.

Approved spending limits

We propose a monthly budget and how to split it across channels. We keep spending within the limit you approve and never raise it without your written approval.

Campaigns and measurement

Hotel Digital Marketing covers campaign structure, weekly bid adjustments, monthly reviews and conversion tracking. If vendor-installed tags are required, we make a test booking within 5 business days. You also get a 30-day report.

Regional, corporate and wholesale channels beyond the major OTAs

We add regional, corporate and wholesale channels only when they suit your source markets and type of guest.

Being available isn't enough of a reason to add a channel. We prepare content and promotions tailored to each channel, including regional platforms such as Traveloka, MakeMyTrip and Despegar. The same goes for HRS, Priceline and Hostelworld. For boutique hotels, we also cover Airbnb.

Through our Hotel Distribution service, we split your inventory across OTAs, the GDS and direct channels based on booking trends and commissions. We also control pricing and availability to keep your rates in parity and lower the risk of overbooking.

Listing content tuned to each platform's ranking factors

  • Platform-specific content

    On Airbnb, Vrbo and other OTAs, we tailor your titles, descriptions, photo order and captions to each platform's ranking and conversion factors, and we optimize all of them for search.

  • Ranking factors

    We work on your guest scores, content scores, room types, amenities, rate plans, policies and promotions, following each platform's own requirements.

  • Placement and reviews

    Listing content is only one of the things that affect your ranking. We also manage your Booking.com placement programmes, your Expedia placement and your review signals.

  • Weekly competitive tracking

    Every week, we track where each of your listings ranks against your competitive set on every channel. That way we act on a falling trend before it hits your bookings.

Rate plans, rate floors and safe mapping changes

Agreed room floors

We agree a rate floor with you for each room type and build your channel rate plans around your base rate. That protects your average rate while offers make you more visible.

Discount stacking checks

Before any seasonal rule, event rule or promotion goes live, we model how Booking.com and Expedia discounts combine for each room type. That includes Genius, member and mobile offers, early-booker deals and last-minute promotions.

Price protection tests

We confirm that no combination of discounts sells below your room floor or your direct rate. We test for parity and wholesale leakage, and we run the stacking checks again whenever a channel adds or changes a promotion.

Second specialist review

First we test each proposed automated rule change against stacked channel discounts. Then a second specialist reviews it before it goes live.

Protected mapping changes

Before we restructure your mapped rooms or rates, we back up your mappings and do the work during low-demand hours. Your existing rooms and rates stay live until their replacements are mapped and selling.

Reservation continuity

Your existing reservations keep their original room and rate. We check every reservation that comes in during a mapping change or migration within 24 hours.

Placement programmes that pay back their commission

  • Commission before placement

    We recommend Preferred Partner or Visibility Booster only when the extra bookings we expect outweigh the added commission, and we calibrate each placement programme to yield.

  • Monthly programme results

    Each month, your report shows how every programme affects your visibility, ADR, RevPAR and revenue. Revenue is shown both gross and net of the added commission.

  • Unprofitable programme action

    We judge every programme on the revenue it brings in after its added commission. If a programme doesn't pay, we scale it back or leave it.

  • Your programme exclusions

    You can exclude Genius, Preferred Partner or Visibility Booster. If you do, we optimize only through content, rate plans, promotions and channel mix.

  • Existing commercial agreements

    Your wholesale contracts, allotments and negotiated rates stay as they are. We change them only with your written approval.

Rate parity and channel mix without undercutting

  • Channel mix and differentials

    What we do
    We decide which OTAs get more weight and when. We manage rate differentials so that no channel suppresses your rank or undercuts your direct rate.

  • Undercutting alert

    What we do
    We trace where the undercutting comes from: wholesale rates leaking to resellers, mapping or markup mistakes, stacked promotions or discounts funded by the OTA.

  • Leaking rates

    What we do
    We shut off or restrict the leaking rate, and we give each channel its own declared markup in your channel manager.

  • OTA-funded discounts

    What we do
    We take the issue up with the OTA so that you have the option to opt out.

  • Verification after changes

    What we do
    After every fix or change, we recheck parity and availability across the platforms it affects. Then we check again at a 30-day review.

  • Direct-booking incentives

    What we do
    We set up member-only rates, value-added packages or extras for booking on your website, all within your parity agreements.

One-time, multi-channel or ongoing OTA management

One-time listing work

One-time optimization is the right choice when one channel needs fixing and your team will run the extranet afterwards. For Booking.com, delivery takes 2 to 3 business days.

Your team's handover

Once we've made the lasting listing changes, your team keeps rates, parity, promotions and programmes such as Genius up to date.

Included follow-up review

Thirty days after the one-time optimization, we review your ranking and conversion and make one round of adjustments at no extra cost.

Periodic listing reviews

We recommend a listing review every 6 to 12 months, because platform algorithms and your hotel's market keep changing.

Multi-channel engagement

If your property sells on 3 or more OTAs, we align your content, pricing, promotions and parity across Agoda, Booking.com, Trip.com, Expedia Group, regional OTAs and metasearch, all at the same time.

Ongoing daily management

If you don't have in-house time for the extranets, we monitor parity and promotions every day. We also update your content, rates and programmes as algorithms and promotions change.

Fully managed extranets

When our team manages the work, you don't need to log into the extranets, and we get your approval of new content before any full rebuild goes live.

Monthly engagement flexibility

Ongoing OTA management runs on a monthly retainer, and you can cancel at the end of any month.

  • Full rebuild fees

How we measure the lift on every channel

Before we change anything, we record a baseline for each channel.

Your before-and-after summary compares every channel with its own baseline, using that extranet's analytics, and includes a parity check across all channels. At the 30-day review, we look at placement, page views, conversion, booking share and review score; for metasearch, we measure direct bookings.

Ask us and we'll share full case studies and references.

We judge channels by net contribution, not gross bookings.

Your monthly report shows occupancy, ADR, RevPAR and each channel's revenue gross and net of cancellations, commissions, programme, payment, advertising and other acquisition costs, plus its cost of sale, against last year, budget and competitive set.

We agree goals for net ADR, RevPAR, direct booking share, channel mix and acquisition cost as a share of room revenue.

Frequently Asked Questions

My property is not listed on Expedia or Agoda yet. Can you create and launch new listings for me, or do you only rebuild listings that already exist?

Yes. We launch new Expedia, Agoda and regional listings, starting from the application and registration, and we create the accounts in your property's name. We prepare your content, photos, policies, rooms and rate plans. We add metasearch and direct booking links, then connect and map the channels through the channel manager you already use, including SiteMinder, Cloudbeds and most other major systems. We negotiate the commission and programme terms, and your team signs the contracts. From you, we need your business registration, evidence of ownership or operating rights, your tax and payout bank details, and named logins with minimum permissions. When there's no booking history, we set your opening rates from your competitive set's rates, market demand and your budget.

Are metasearch connectivity and paid campaign management included in the OTA optimization fee, or separately priced additions?

Ongoing OTA management includes live rate and availability connections to Google free booking links and metasearch. Managing paid Google Hotel Ads and other metasearch campaigns is priced separately, and we agree that price with you in writing before work starts. Neither fee covers media spend. You pay that directly to the platforms.

Can I limit the engagement to selected channels, for example only Booking.com and Expedia, and add others later?

Yes. You choose the channels, and you can add others later. We recommend starting with the ones that already bring you the most bookings. Each extra launch is one-off setup work, and we quote it before it starts. Your monthly retainer stays the same.

Can you restrict your work to listing content and distribution while our existing revenue manager retains all base-rate and stay-restriction decisions?

Yes. With standalone OTA management, we cover content, rate plan structure, promotions, parity and channel manager settings, and your revenue manager keeps every base-rate and stay-restriction decision. We agree in writing who owns which channels and tasks. We never change your revenue manager's rates or restrictions. When we spot an issue, we flag it for them to decide.

Can you manage several OTA accounts directly if we have no channel manager, or is installing one a prerequisite?

Yes. We work directly in each extranet and update rates, availability and content by hand. We use named logins, and you remove them when we finish. You don't need a channel manager. If you sell on several OTAs, we recommend one because it reduces the risk of overbooking. We help you choose and connect it, and we take no referral commission from the vendor.

Will you talk to my Booking.com, Expedia or Agoda market managers on my behalf about commission, programme terms or contract conditions?

Yes. We negotiate those matters with your market managers and push for performance incentives or volume-based commission reductions. You approve every proposal first, and you sign any new contract yourself.

Do you commit to any target for ranking, conversion or booking share, and what do you do if the 30-day review shows no improvement?

We agree measurable goals for ranking, conversion and booking share with you, but we don't guarantee specific revenue, occupancy or ADR results. If the 30-day review shows no improvement, we make one round of adjustments free of charge. If targets are missed 2 months in a row, we give you a written recovery plan. You keep the right to cancel at the end of any month.

How long does setup take for a full multi-channel engagement, including the Expedia and Agoda rebuilds and the metasearch connection?

A full multi-channel setup takes 2 to 3 weeks. Within that time, Booking.com optimization takes 2 to 3 business days, and each Expedia Group or Agoda rebuild takes 5 to 7 business days. Metasearch connections take 7 to 10 business days and run alongside the OTA work.
