Why Join RateHarbor?

You'll join a 12-person team that works fully remotely.

You own results for hotel owners, and you work inside each client's own systems. We provide revenue management, OTA management and owner reporting for independent hotels and small hotel groups in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia and other countries.

We're looking for people who enjoy working with hotel data and online channels.

How Our Remote Team Works

The team has three groups: revenue management, OTA management and owner reporting.

Each account has one dedicated specialist, so owners always deal with someone they know. When you're on vacation or in training, colleagues who know the account cover your properties. Pricing and listings need attention every day, so part of your week goes on handover notes and shared updates.

Some people find this coordination work less rewarding than direct client work. Your core hours are 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time, Monday to Friday. You get 20 paid vacation days a year and weekly internal training and knowledge-sharing sessions.

We also give you paid time to take revenue management certification courses.

The Roles We Hire For

Our roles all cover the same properties, but each is narrower than a generalist hotel job. Pick the one matching your strongest interest; you'll serve clients better.

  • Revenue Management Specialists

    This is the role for you if you want to own pricing decisions. You handle pricing and rate strategy, demand forecasting and daily competitive set checks, and you own up to 10 client properties from start to finish.

  • OTA Management Specialists

    This is the role for you if you want to work on channel operations. You run listings, content, rate parity and channel manager settings on Booking.com, Expedia and other online travel agencies.

  • Owner Reporting Analysts

    This is the role for you if you want to turn performance data into reports. You build weekly and monthly owner reports covering occupancy, ADR and RevPAR.

How to Apply: From Application to Offer

Job titles vary, so we judge your practical work, not just your CV, across three video-call steps over about 3 weeks.

  1. 01

    Send Your Application

    Email your CV and a short note saying which role you want to the address on our contact page.

  2. 02

    Introductory Video Call

    The video-call process starts with a 30-minute introductory call, which comes before the practical exercise.

  3. 03

    Practical Property Exercise

    You spend 2 hours on pricing or listings for a sample property. These 2 hours show us how you work more clearly than an interview answer does.

  4. 04

    Final Team Interview

    After you finish the practical exercise, you meet two team members for the final interview.

  5. 05

    Decision Or Offer

    Within 5 business days of the final interview, we send you a decision or a written offer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you hire people with no experience in the hotel industry?

Yes. For owner reporting analyst and junior OTA roles, you don't need hotel industry experience as long as you have at least 2 years of work in data analysis, e-commerce or online marketing. We train you over your first 3 months. Specialist roles that own client accounts are different: for those, you need at least 5 years of hands-on hotel revenue management or OTA experience.

Do you offer internships or student placements?

Yes. Each year we offer up to 2 paid remote placements for students in hospitality, tourism, business or data analytics programmes, and each one lasts 3 to 6 months. You work alongside a specialist on competitive set monitoring, listing content and performance reports for hotels, motels, B&Bs, serviced apartments and short-term rentals.

Will you keep my application on file if no role fits right now?

Yes, if you ask for this in the note with your CV. We keep it only to consider you for future roles, and if a role that matches your experience opens, we contact you directly. You can ask us to update or delete your application at any time, and we act on your request within 5 business days.

How do you set salaries and review pay?

When we advertise an open role, its posting includes the salary range. With no open role, your application joins our talent pool, and we tell you the range before any interview. We set pay by level across three bands: associate, specialist and senior, and an offer sits within that range. Fixed bands support fair pay across our remote team. The trade-off is that you have less room to negotiate. If you're based in Canada, we pay you in Canadian dollars. If you're on the remote team somewhere else, we pay you in your local currency, so take-home pay isn't identical from one country to another. We review pay for everyone on the team every 12 months.
