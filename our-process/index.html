Who Runs Your Account and How Often We Check Rates

Your named specialist

One dedicated specialist owns your account from start to finish and makes the daily pricing decisions. Our wider revenue team backs them up, and you get weekly or monthly strategy calls and coverage on all 365 days of the year.

Continuity and fit

Your specialist keeps track of your property's demand context, so decisions stay consistent. When they're away, our team covers so pricing carries on. If the relationship isn't working, ask for a replacement early so the mismatch doesn't drag on.

Dynamic Pricing Strategy

On the monthly retainer, Dynamic Pricing Strategy means we manage daily rates, restrictions and inventory for every room type and adjust them to live demand and market conditions.

Remote Work Inside Your Existing Systems

We work remotely inside the PMS, channel manager and RMS you already use, through our own logins, so you don't need replacement software.

We don't have on-site staff at any property. Staying with your current systems means you avoid the rate and inventory risks and the staff retraining that come with switching mid-season. The value comes from our decisions, not from a new tool.

What your systems can do sets the limit: if your setup is restricted, some automation isn't possible until you upgrade.

Reports, Strategy Calls and Your Control Over Rates

Weekly and monthly performance reviews

We walk your team through the results on your weekly or monthly strategy call. Owners and GMs get weekly and monthly reports covering RevPAR growth, occupancy trends, ADR movement, visibility, revenue and forecast accuracy.

Leadership reporting

Our monthly report sets out the key performance metrics, and every quarter you get an in-depth review. Senior leadership also gets regular financial performance reports and forecasts.

Live KPI access

Live dashboards let you check occupancy, ADR, RevPAR and channel mix at any time, so you can see how you're performing between reviews.

Strategy call schedule

We hold strategy calls weekly or monthly to go over results and direction. More frequent calls and reports take up more owner and GM time, so we set the schedule to match how involved your leadership wants to be.

Your pricing control

You keep full control of pricing and can see every rate and channel. We make major decisions together with you and keep disruption to your team to a minimum.

Check before signing

Before you sign, we send you an anonymised sample monthly report. Look at how it explains rate changes, results and forecasts against actuals, not just the KPIs. That shows you how you'll hold us accountable.

Sample daily snapshot

You also get a sample daily snapshot. It shows changes from the last 24 hours, a day-by-day view of the next 12 months and competitors' rates for context.

What We Need From You: Input, Approvals and Access

Name one contact who approves quickly.

Join weekly or monthly strategy calls, answer approvals within 2 business days and keep active named logins for your PMS, channel manager, RMS and OTA extranets; lapsed access or late approvals stop work.

Give us 14 days' notice of renovations, rooms out of service, group blocks, events or budget changes. Our data handling policy follows PIPEDA, plus GDPR and UK GDPR where they apply.

How the Work Begins, Including Multi-Property Groups

  1. 01

    Start without commitment

    Before any engagement, you get a free 30-minute consultation and free quote, and you don't have to commit to going ahead.

  2. 02

    Audit before targets

Frequently Asked Questions

How does this work for a seasonal property that closes for part of the year?

While you're closed, we pause daily pricing. We still check your listings and reply to new reviews every month. We close off-season dates on every channel. We also set opening rates and minimum stays based on past seasons, comp-set rates and market demand, so guests can book up to 12 months ahead. Full pricing starts again 8 weeks before you reopen.

How do you work alongside a revenue manager or front desk staff I already have?

At the start, we agree in writing who owns which channels and tasks. Your staff keep check-in, guest service, walk-ins and direct bookings. We handle pricing, restrictions and OTA listings remotely. We email you the current rate plan, and we email every change on the day it goes live. At launch, we also give a 30-minute remote briefing. That keeps your staff and channels aligned with minimal disruption.

Who makes the final call on rates, you or me?

You keep full control of pricing. We make major decisions together, and you can see every rate and channel while we keep disruption to your team to a minimum.

What do you do between reviews if something sudden happens, like a local event or a drop in bookings?

We check rates every day and change prices in real time. We base those changes on demand, booking pace, live pickup, seasonality, events, competitor benchmarks and market trends. On the monthly retainer, we decide rates, restrictions and inventory for every room type and revise the strategy as conditions change. When demand is slow, we use targeted tactics to fill unsold rooms. At peak demand, the aim shifts to the highest ADR the market will accept. We check your OTAs every day for slower bookings and broken rate parity, track your listings' ranking every week, and fix any problem we find.
