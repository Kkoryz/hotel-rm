Who Our Hotel Resources Are For

Our resources let you review room pricing, OTA listings, budgets and performance reporting on your own, before or between projects with us. They're built for owners and operators of independent hotels and small hotel groups, including hotels, motels, boutique hotels, B&Bs, serviced apartments and short-term rentals.

Guides, Templates & Checklists for Hotel Owners

Rate strategy guide

We walk you through base rates, rate floors, seasonal and event adjustments, and length-of-stay rules so you can make pricing decisions for your property. Its first section sets a rate floor from your cost per occupied room.

OTA listing checklist

The checklist covers your titles, descriptions, photos, room types, policies and rate parity on Booking.com and Expedia, with checks such as "Same rate and policy on both sites for this date and room?"

Annual budget template

We break your annual budget down by month and by segment, so you can work from either view: months run across, segments down, with room nights, ADR and room revenue.

Monthly performance report

The report tracks occupancy, ADR, RevPAR and channel mix. When you use it alongside the rate guide, you measure your pricing decisions and your results against the same figures.

Each month you can see whether your rules are doing what you set them up to do.

Monthly time commitment

To use the paired tools properly, someone who knows your property's numbers has to spend time on them every month. If you run a very small property without a dedicated manager, keeping up that commitment is harder.

How to Get Each Resource & When to Use It

If you tie each resource to its decision deadline, you'll have time to act before demand, OTA rankings or budgets are locked in.

  • Free resource access

    Every guide, template and checklist is free, and each card above shows a sample of it. Request the ones you want on the form, and we'll email the full files.

  • Peak season pricing

    Use the rate strategy guide 8 to 12 weeks before your peak season, because that's when it's most useful.

  • Year-round demand

    If your demand is fairly even with no clear peak, seasonal timing gives you less value. In that case, review your rates on a steady rolling schedule.

  • Listing refresh preparation

    Go through the OTA listing checklist before any listing refresh and use it to guide your changes.

  • Financial year preparation

    Use the budget template about 3 months before your financial year starts.

  • Month-end reporting

    Use the performance report template at each month-end close to review your results.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often are the guides and checklists updated?

We review every guide and checklist each quarter. Whenever Booking.com or Expedia changes its listing or ranking rules, we update the OTA listing checklist within 30 days. Outdated guidance can harm your visibility, so always use the latest version.

Can I adapt the templates and share them with my staff?

Yes. You can edit any template, add your own room types and figures, and share it with staff and general managers at every property you run. You can't resell the files or republish them as your own.

Do I need any particular software to open or use the templates?

No, you don't need any special software. The templates are Excel (.xlsx) files that open in Excel, Google Sheets, Apple Numbers and LibreOffice. The guides and checklists are PDFs that open in any browser. You don't need any new systems, and there are no setup costs. The templates aren't connected to a PMS, channel manager or pricing tool, so you enter or paste your figures by hand. The more properties you have, the more work this takes.

Can I ask someone a question if a guide doesn't fit my property?

Yes. Send our team an enquiry about your property and the guide. We'll answer your question about the guide.
