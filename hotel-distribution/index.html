Distribution Strategy Built Around Your Hotel

We build your hotel distribution strategy around your property. We place your inventory across online travel agencies and your own direct booking channels to grow your online presence.

Channel Distribution Across OTAs, Wholesale and New Listings

Wholesale rate protection

We choose your wholesale and bedbank partners and set net rates with package-only or closed-user-group restrictions, so resale prices stay above your direct rate. Then we check where those rates show up online.

Your contract control

Your existing contracts, allotments and negotiated rates stay as they are. We change them only with your written approval, and you sign every new contract yourself.

Selected OTA support

Through our OTA Optimization service, we manage the channels you choose as a standalone service. We launch new listings, fix parity issues and review the fixes after 30 days, and we send you monthly performance reports.

We agree with you in writing who owns each channel and task.

Rate Parity Across Channels and Direct Booking Growth

Public rate parity

We keep your public direct rate in line with your OTA rates instead of openly undercutting them.

Direct booking benefits

Within your parity agreements, we give guests reasons to book directly: member-only rates, direct-only perks and added value.

Existing booking engine

We keep your existing booking engine and maintain your rates, availability, rate plans and offers inside it. We connect metasearch and direct booking links to it without replacing it.

Website booking growth

Our Hotel Digital Marketing service brings more visitors to your website and turns more of them into guests, which cuts your OTA commission costs.

Allocating Room Inventory by Booking Data and Commission Cost

We use your booking data and commission costs to balance what your rooms earn against your exposure on each channel.

How we allocate your rooms across channels

  1. 01

    Study booking trends, commissions

    We look at your booking trends and the commission each channel charges.

  2. 02

    Prioritise revenue-supporting channels

    We put first the channels that support your revenue goals and earn you the most.

  3. 03

    Spread channel exposure evenly

    We keep your exposure spread evenly so that no single channel carries too much of it.

  4. 04

    Review split regularly

    We check the split against booking pace and season on a regular basis instead of setting it once.

Net booking value

We rank your channels by net revenue per booking, not by volume. That figure is what's left after commissions, discounts, cancellations and refunds. We also balance your non-refundable rates against your flexible ones.

When a channel has heavy cancellations or deep promotions, it drops in the ranking.

Promotion rate floors

We build your member, mobile, early-booker and last-minute offers around your base rate. Before any offer goes live, we check that stacked discounts never take the price below your rate floor or your direct rate.

Wider cost review

Our Revenue Management Audit looks at your payment costs, commissions, brand and loyalty fees, distribution and sales costs, marketing spend and commercial terms. It then shows your net contribution by channel, segment and revenue source.

We move only inventory that sits outside your contracted allotments, release periods and exclusivity commitments.

What happens to contracted allotments

  1. 01

    Contracts stay fixed limits

    Contracted allotments, release periods and exclusivity commitments stay as they are unless you approve a change in writing.

  2. 02

    Release dates added

    We put every allotment's release date into the selling calendar.

  3. 03

    Unsold rooms return

    Once an allotment passes its release date, its unsold rooms go back to the shared pool.

  4. 04

    Offered on higher-yield channels

    Rooms that come back at release are offered on your higher-yield channels straight away.

One live inventory pool

Your OTAs and your direct booking engine all draw from one shared room count through your channel manager. There are no separate OTA blocks or manual extranet updates, so your overbooking risk stays low.

Near sell-out controls

When a date gets close to selling out, we close your highest-commission channels first with stop-sells and closed-to-arrival restrictions. Your direct channel stays open until the last room.

Pricing integration safeguards

When you also use our Dynamic Pricing Strategy, we pause automation as soon as there's a sync fault.

We fix the fault within 4 hours and tell you about it the same day. If more than 12 hours pass without fresh data, we hold your verified rates.

After syncing is restored, we reconcile any bookings made during the outage within 24 hours.

Frequently Asked Questions

Beyond the published room-count tiers, do the number of managed channels, new-channel launches or inclusion of GDS and wholesale distribution change your service fee?

No. The number of channels we manage, including GDS and wholesale, doesn't change your monthly retainer, which is set by your room-count tier. We also take no commission on your room revenue. Launching a new channel or connecting you to the GDS carries a separate one-off fee. After a free 30-minute consultation, we quote you a fixed launch fee based on your room types and channels, and we agree it with you in writing. You pay GDS fees, wholesale commissions and any other third-party costs directly to the providers.

What access and documents do I need to give you before you can take over my distribution?

Give each of our specialists their own named login with minimum permissions to your PMS, channel manager, booking engine, RMS and OTA extranets. We never work from a shared password. Send us your property details, at least 12 months of booking history and a list of your channel, wholesale, GDS and corporate contracts with their commission terms, allotments and negotiated rates. We go through that list with you on a 60-minute kickoff call.

If I'm not on the GDS yet, can you get me connected and handle corporate RFPs and negotiated rates?

Yes. We connect you to the GDS through your channel manager in your property’s name. We then add GDS and corporate business to your channel mix with its own markup against your direct rate. We prepare and submit your corporate and consortia RFP responses, propose negotiated rates for you to approve and load the rates you agree to. You sign any new GDS or programme contracts yourself.
