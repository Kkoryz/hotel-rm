Digital Marketing Built Around Your Guests and Occupancy

Every week, we point promotions and advertising at stay dates that are forecast below 70% occupancy or are pacing behind last year.

Your occupancy forecast, booking pace and open room inventory decide where our hotel digital marketing puts that spend. Once a date passes 90% forecast occupancy, or fewer than 3 rooms per type remain, we cut or pause advertising for that date and let pricing capture the demand.

We build your strategy around your hotel and the guests you want to attract. We map how those guests book online and optimise your digital channels around those sales funnels.

Your Digital Marketing Agency, Consultant or Interim Lead

At the start, we put in writing who owns each channel and each task.

Consulting or interim leadership

With marketing consulting, we provide strategy, recommendations and training, and your team runs the campaigns. As your interim marketing lead for 3 to 6 months, we set priorities, brief your staff and agencies, and review their work.

Managed marketing delivery

We build, run and report on your campaigns ourselves. Your team keeps the final say on everything that gets published.

Revenue before traffic

We judge your marketing on direct booking revenue after cancellations, commissions and acquisition costs, not on traffic or gross bookings.

Every report leads with direct booking revenue and shows it next to media spend, conversion rate, cost per direct booking and the OTA commission you avoided.

Channel performance comparisons

For each channel, we show you revenue both gross and net of OTA commissions, advertising, booking-engine and other acquisition costs, compared with last year, your budget and your competitive set.

That way you can tell real net growth apart from bookings that just moved between channels, and the effect of marketing apart from the hotel's overall performance.

Audit and strategy deliverables

Our audit gives you written findings and an action plan that ranks fixes by expected impact and effort. The strategy adds a 12-month campaign calendar and a recommended budget split across paid search, metasearch, SEO, email and social media.

Our company background

At RateHarbor, we provide hotel owners with outsourced hotel revenue management and OTA management services.

Boutique hotel consulting

Boutique Hotel Consulting focuses your marketing on retail and leisure direct bookings. We advise on group strategy while your sales director handles day-to-day sales.

For openings and repositioning, we support you up to 6 months before launch and use market data until your own bookings and guest list build up.

A Brand and Website Built to Win Direct Bookings

Consistent hotel identity

We develop your hotel's brand identity and messaging, so guests see your property presented the same way on every channel.

More direct guests

We audit and optimise your existing website or build you a direct-booking site. That brings in more visitors and turns more of them into guests, so you pay less in OTA commissions.

Improve sound foundations

If your current site loads on mobile in under 3 seconds, its CMS can still be updated and its booking-engine connection works cleanly, we improve the site you have.

The speed, room-page, call-to-action and booking-path fixes then go live within 4 to 6 weeks.

Rebuild obstructive platforms

If your site isn't mobile-friendly, can't hold a booking widget or needs more than half its pages redone, we recommend a rebuild. The extra work lets us redesign the whole booking journey, which gives you a bigger lift in conversion.

Booking engine included

A new website comes with choosing, setting up and tuning your booking engine, or with connecting the engine you already use. We don't just add a booking link.

Reservations tested before launch

We make test reservations on desktop and mobile and follow each one through to the confirmation emails. We check rates and availability against your channel manager, then cancel every test booking.

Find booking drop-offs

Using your website analytics and booking-engine reports, we trace your booking journey from date search to payment and find where visitors leave.

Test conversion improvements

We test changes like fewer form fields, clearer rates or more visible booking buttons, and we run each version for at least 4 weeks or 1,000 visits. We keep the version that converts better and then fix the next drop-off.

Google Ads, Metasearch and SEO in Accounts You Own

  • Google Ads campaigns

    We plan your digital advertising and run Google Ads campaigns that send visitors to your hotel's website.

  • Hotel metasearch campaigns

    We also run hotel metasearch campaigns as part of your digital advertising programme.

  • Brand bidding tests

    In selected periods, we pause your brand campaigns for 2 to 4 weeks and compare total direct bookings. This tells us whether bidding wins reservations you would otherwise lose to resellers.

  • Spend follows booking value

    We keep brand bidding only where resellers are bidding. Where your organic listings win the bookings, we reduce or stop brand spend and move that money into non-brand search and metasearch.

  • Your advertising accounts

    Your ad and metasearch accounts are opened in your property's name and stay yours. You pay the platforms directly with your own payment method.

  • Your digital assets

    Your website, domain and guest email lists are registered to you or kept in your systems. You own all the content and accounts we build.

  • Files handed over

    Within 10 business days after our engagement ends, we give your team the current files.

Search foundations

We plan and run SEO for your hotel's name, your visibility on Google Maps and your website's technical requirements.

Destination and property searches

We also target non-brand searches for your destination and property type, and each target search gets its own landing page. You get 3 to 5 pages in the first 3 months, and we add more as new opportunities come up.

OTA and metasearch visibility

Through OTA Optimization, we aim for a 100% listing content score. We send your rates and availability directly to Google free booking links, Hotel Ads, trivago, TripAdvisor and Kayak, and we manage your Google Business Profile and your reviews.

Distribution and channel returns

Through Hotel Distribution, we allocate your inventory across OTAs, the GDS and direct channels based on booking trends and returns after commission.

You keep your third-party visibility and bookings, and we control pricing and availability to lower your overbooking risk and keep rate parity.

Email, Content and Social Media That Bring Guests Back

Guest segments and sequences

We grow your guest email list and divide it into segments. We time campaigns to booking windows and build upselling and automated sequences that fill quiet periods without cutting rates.

Campaign creation and approval

We write and design each email campaign and send it from your own platform once you've approved it in writing.

Testing and monthly reporting

We test subject lines and send times and report your opens, clicks and bookings each month. If your team runs email instead, we give you the strategy and templates.

Social media management

We manage organic posts and community replies on Facebook, Instagram and X (Twitter). We also run paid social campaigns in your property's ad accounts, and you pay the media spend directly.

Monthly content volume

Each month, we produce 2 website articles of about 1,000 words, 12 social posts and 2 videos of up to 60 seconds. You can commission extra blocks of the same size.

Remote video production

We only work remotely. We edit videos from footage and photos you send us or from a shoot you arrange locally.

Influencer delivery and tracking

We shortlist creators and agree with each one which posts, stories or videos they'll deliver.

Then we check what they publish against those commitments and track the bookings that come from it through a unique booking link or promo code for each creator.

Hosted stays and approval

Your property hosts each influencer stay, and you approve every creator before we confirm what they've agreed to deliver.

Independent Hotels and Small Groups We Work With

We work with independent hotels and small hotel groups. We manage your website, booking engine, digital campaigns and email platforms together as one direct-booking system. Just ask and we'll share our full case studies.

Booking Tracking That Credits Every Reservation

Before we increase your ad spend, we check that completed, uncancelled reservations can be traced back to the campaigns that produced them. We don't judge performance on clicks alone.

How we connect every completed booking to the campaign that brought the guest in.

  1. Named login access

    We work through a named login in your own analytics, advertising and booking-engine accounts.

  2. Cross-domain GA4 tracking

    We set up cross-domain tracking in Google Analytics 4 between your website and your booking engine.

  3. Conversions to ad platforms

    We pass booking-confirmation conversions to Google Ads and metasearch, and link each metasearch platform's tracking to your booking engine, with cancellations taken out.

  4. Test booking confirmed

    If your booking engine vendor has to install the tags, we provide the code and confirm a test booking within 5 business days.

  5. 30-day check report

    We report impressions, clicks, completed direct bookings and the commission saved compared with getting the same bookings through OTAs.

Works With Your CMS, PMS and Email Platform

Your existing systems

We work inside your CMS, whether that's WordPress, Squarespace or Wix. We also work with your existing booking engine, PMS and channel manager, including SiteMinder and Cloudbeds. We don't replace any of your systems.

Email platform options

We set up and manage Mailchimp, Klaviyo, HubSpot, Brevo or a comparable platform. We can also use the email tool or CRM you already have, or your PMS's guest CRM module if your PMS has one.

Connected guest records

We connect the guest data in your PMS to your email platform through the platform's native integration or a scheduled export.

Access and data protection

Your guest data stays in your systems. It's covered by your NDA or our data processing agreement, and that agreement is signed before we receive access.

Frequently Asked Questions

What will my team need to give you, such as account access and brand assets? And which ads and content do I have to approve, and how often?

You'll give us your property details, logo, brand guidelines and photo library. You'll also give us user access to your website, booking engine, email platform and the ad accounts your property owns, and join us for a 60-minute kickoff call. No ad, listing text or other content goes live until you've approved it in writing. We send you one batch to approve each week and ask you to reply within 2 business days.

Which choices change your marketing service fee: managed channels, media spend, content volume, website scope, integrations or language versions?

Your fixed monthly retainer depends on how many channels we manage and how much content we produce each month. Website projects, system integrations and extra language versions are one-time fees, set out in your written proposal. Media spend doesn't change our fee. You pay ad and metasearch platforms directly with your own payment method, separately from our fee, and we take no percentage.

Can we commission selected marketing channels without buying the full marketing service or your revenue management service?

Yes. You can commission paid search, SEO, email or any other single channel without taking full marketing or revenue management. Revenue management, OTA management and our related online services are available separately or together. If you choose several, we bundle them and give you priority turnaround.

If you build me a new direct-booking website, how long until it is live, and how soon do campaigns start?

We launch a new direct-booking website, or updates to your existing site, in 4 to 6 weeks. Nothing is published until you've signed it off in writing. Ad campaigns start within 10 business days of us getting account access and your approval. During the build, we can run them against your current site and redirect them on launch day. Paid search brings in visitors from the first day. Most hotels see measurable results from paid search and SEO within 60 to 90 days, and SEO rankings usually keep building over 6 to 12 months, but we don't guarantee specific results.

What minimum media budget is needed for you to manage paid search or metasearch meaningfully, and which would you recommend if we cannot fund both?

For paid search, your budget needs to cover about 30 clicks a day on your key search terms. For metasearch, it needs to keep bids live every day across at least 90 days of stay dates. We launch either channel only when the funding keeps it running without gaps. If you fund one channel, metasearch comes first and paid search second. Metasearch reaches travellers who are already comparing rates, and your spend follows actual clicks or bookings. You pay media spend directly to each platform, outside our fee.

Can you take over our existing advertising accounts and campaigns while preserving their history and working around channels still managed by our current agency?

Yes. You add a named login for us to your existing ad and metasearch accounts, which preserves your campaign history, conversion data and bidding history. We review the accounts for 1 to 2 weeks before we change anything. Where your agency keeps some channels, we agree in writing at the start who owns each channel and task, and we work alongside them to minimise disruption.

Which languages can you support for campaigns, website content and guest emails, including localisation rather than translation alone?

We create campaigns, website content and guest emails in both English and French. We write and adapt each one for its market instead of translating word for word. For other languages, you supply the translations, and we adapt the layouts, currencies, date formats and offers to the market.

After launch or optimisation, do you maintain website content, software and booking links, or must our team or web developer handle those tasks?

While your monthly programme runs, we maintain your website through the user access you give us. That covers up to 4 content updates a month, software and plugin updates within 5 business days of their release, and a check of your booking-engine links once a week. If you keep a developer or agency, we agree in writing at the start who owns each task.
