An Outsourced Revenue Management Company for Hotel Owners

You get an outsourced revenue team, not software you have to run yourself.

At RateHarbor, we provide hotel revenue management and OTA management for hotel owners. Our people study your data, build your strategy and do the day-to-day work. You can hire us for either service on its own or combine them with related online services.

We handle your revenue and online distribution work. We don't run your hotel operations. We shape your service around your property, and it grows with you as you add rooms or properties.

How We Work as Your Remote Revenue Management Consultant

Experienced account ownership

Our Canadian team has 12 people. Your dedicated specialist has at least 5 years of revenue and OTA experience with independent and boutique hotels and manages no more than 10 properties.

The team supports them all 365 days of the year.

Remote working rhythm

Through our remote process, we work directly in your hotel's systems and online accounts, with no on-site staff. We make pricing decisions every day and go through strategy with you on a weekly or monthly call.

Evidence before commitment

If you ask before you sign, we share full case studies showing before-and-after RevPAR, occupancy and ADR for properties of a similar type and size. We also share an anonymised monthly performance report.

Controlled data access

Under our data safeguards, we sign your NDA or our processing agreement before we get any access. We apply PIPEDA, plus GDPR and UK GDPR where they apply.

Guest data stays in your systems, only our team can access cloud working files, and we don't use subcontractors.

Flexible managed plans

Under our plan terms, you can stop any managed plan at the end of any month if you tell us in writing before that month ends. There's no long-term lock-in and there are no cancellation fees.

If you cancel during onboarding, you pay only for the onboarding work we've already completed.

Revenue Management Services, from Daily Pricing to Owner Reports

We take responsibility for your revenue work from strategy through implementation and ongoing optimisation, so you don't have to build an in-house revenue team.

Strategy agreed with you

We build your revenue strategy around your hotel or resort and the occupancy, ADR and RevPAR goals you set. Then we turn our findings into improvement plans you can act on.

Clear decision boundaries

On the monthly retainer, we decide daily rates, restrictions and inventory within guardrails we agree with you.

You keep full control of pricing, see every rate and channel and make major decisions together with us, and we never overwrite a manual rate override you set.

Competitive market monitoring

We track your competitive set alongside market trends. That way your pricing and rate strategy is based on competitor benchmarks and demand signals instead of your property's performance in isolation.

Daily pricing

On the monthly retainer, we decide rates, restrictions and inventory for every room type, using the pricing approach set out in Dynamic Pricing Strategy. We respond in real time to pickup, booking pace, seasonality, events and demand.

We use targeted tactics when demand is low and set ADR goals for peak demand.

Hotel Distribution

With Hotel Distribution, we allocate your inventory across OTAs, the GDS and direct channels, weighing booking trends against commissions. We control availability and pricing to keep rate parity and reduce your overbooking risk.

OTA listing management

Our OTA management service runs your listings and rates on Booking.com, Expedia and other online travel agencies. This covers your listing content, rate parity and channel manager settings.

  • Property-based demand forecasts

    We project your occupancy and revenue from your booking history, current pace and market trends. These projections take into account demand, seasonality and where you stand against competitors.

  • Weekly owner reporting

    Every week, you get pace and pickup reports that compare bookings with last year and with budget. You also get demand forecast reports and a pace analysis dashboard.

  • Forecast checks and corrections

    We deliver monthly and quarterly forecasts, analyse pickup trends and regularly compare actual results with forecasts. When demand runs ahead of or behind forecast, we correct your pricing and plans.

  • Historical data limits

    Forecast accuracy depends on clean historical data. If your property is new or has poor records, your first projections are less reliable.

  • Commercial Strategy budgets

    With Commercial Strategy, we build your annual budget by month and segment using planning tools and annual templates. Then we maintain and update your budgets and reporting.

  • Standalone fixed-scope, fixed-fee audits

    We quote Revenue Management Audits after your free 30-minute consultation. For a single property, we deliver within 10 business days of getting your data and access. Each batch of up to 5 properties adds another 10 business days. Remote training and handover are included.

  • Room-count pricing tiers

    Our monthly revenue management plans are priced by room count: 40 rooms or fewer, 41 to 120 rooms, and more than 120 rooms.

Properties We Support, from Motels to Growing Hotel Groups

Independent and boutique hotels

We build your strategy around your type of property. Demand patterns, length of stay and channel mix differ between independent hotels, boutique hotels and resorts, so one standard approach leaves revenue on the table.

Growing hotel groups

We take your group's decision-making structure into account, as well as its properties. A tailored strategy takes more setup and discovery work at the start than applying a standard template.

Lodging beyond traditional hotels

We work with any kind of lodging property, including motels, B&Bs, serviced apartments and short-term rentals. Our service isn't limited to larger hotels.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you take over pricing and distribution day to day, or do you advise my own staff who keep doing it?

We take over the day-to-day work as your outsourced revenue management and OTA management team. Our revenue management service covers pricing and rate strategy, demand forecasting, competitive set monitoring and regular performance reports for you as the owner. Our OTA management service runs your listings and rates on Booking.com, Expedia and other online travel agencies. That includes your content, rate parity and channel manager settings.

Do you work with small independent motels, or only with larger branded hotels?

Yes, we work with small independent motels as well as larger hotels. We support any kind of lodging property, including hotels, boutique hotels, B&Bs, serviced apartments and short-term rentals.

Do you take on properties anywhere, or only in certain regions?

We work in many countries, including the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and Australia, so our service isn't tied to one region. We support independent hotels and small hotel groups entirely remotely. We work in your hotel's own systems and online accounts, and we have no on-site staff at any property.

Do you also handle operations like housekeeping and staffing, or only revenue?

We handle revenue management, OTA management and related online services. We don't handle hotel operations such as housekeeping or staffing. You can hire us for revenue management or OTA management on its own, or use both together.
