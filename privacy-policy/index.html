Who We Are and How We Handle Your Data

RateHarbor runs outsourced hotel revenue management and OTA management for hotel owners.

Before we get any access, we sign your NDA or our data processing agreement. We handle personal data under Canada's PIPEDA. Where your guests' data falls under the GDPR or UK GDPR, we handle it under those as well.

Your guest data stays in your own PMS, channel manager and OTA accounts. Our enquiry form also lets you add, if you choose, the service you want, a target date and a reference link such as your website. We use them only to prepare our reply.

Data Security, Access Controls and Vendor Reviews

  • Remote access setup

    Our team works remotely, using named logins with least-privilege access. Your IT team sets up the named accounts and remote access. If that setup is slow, your engagement starts later.

  • Working file storage

    We keep our working files in access-controlled cloud storage that only our own team can use.

  • How long we keep it

    We keep enquiries, resource requests and CVs only while we need them to reply, work with you, send a resource or consider you for a role, then delete them.

  • Who can reach your data

    No subcontractor or freelancer sees your data. Our cloud storage provider hosts our working files, which only our team can open; your own platforms process guest data under your contracts.

  • Written security controls

    During procurement, we give you a written summary of our login, access and file storage controls for your security review.

  • Vendor security questionnaire

    We complete your vendor security questionnaire within 10 business days to support your procurement review.

  • Annual vendor audit

    We accept one vendor audit a year when you give us 30 days' written notice.

  • Who we serve

    We're a Canadian business-to-business service company. We work for hotel owners and operators, not travellers, and you agree every engagement directly with our team.
