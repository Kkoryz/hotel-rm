Consulting services built for owners and small groups.

We work directly with you, the owner or owner-operator, and coordinate with your general manager so execution stays aligned with the plan. Our boutique hotel consulting is for single independent hotels and small groups. When repositioning involves physical changes, we work alongside your own designers and architects.

Working Directly With Owners and Their Teams

Your dedicated specialist

Your specialist owns your account from start to finish and has at least 5 years' experience in independent and boutique hotel revenue and OTAs.

They handle no more than 10 properties and are part of our 12-person team, which provides coverage all 365 days of the year.

Retail and leisure focus

We focus our marketing on retail and leisure direct bookings because that's where our work has the greatest effect. If groups, events or corporate contracts drive most of your revenue, we shape the work around supporting your director of sales.

Group sales ownership

We advise on group strategy, and your director of sales runs group sales day to day. Those relationships and negotiations belong inside your hotel.

Revenue Management Audit

We quote fixed-scope, fixed-fee revenue management audits and roadmaps after your free 30-minute consultation. A standalone audit doesn't commit you to continuing.

For a single property, you get the report within 10 business days of us receiving your data and access. For a group, add 10 business days for each batch of up to 5 properties.

Direct Distribution That Wins Guests Back From OTAs

  • Reasons to book direct

    Guests who find you through OTAs need a clear advantage for booking direct and an easy checkout. We recommend breakfast, late check-out or flexible cancellation. We help you choose the ones your team can deliver consistently at a cost per stay that works for you.

  • Booking engine setup

    We draw on our experience with premium booking engines to select, configure and tune yours, so your direct-booking benefits stay clear all the way through checkout.

  • Returning guest demand

    We build your loyalty and repeat-guest programme and email past guests at the times they usually book to bring them back to you directly.

  • Hotel Distribution

    With hotel distribution, we use booking trends and commissions to allocate your inventory across OTAs, GDS and direct channels. You keep your visibility on third-party sites while we control pricing and availability to reduce the risk of overbooking and preserve parity.

  • OTA Optimization

    Through OTA optimization, we refine your listings, rate plans and promotions, set up your channel-manager mapping, maintain parity, plan commissions and channel mix, and add metasearch and direct-booking links.

  • Hotel Digital Marketing

    As part of hotel digital marketing, we audit and optimise your existing website, or we build a new direct-booking website if you need one.

Weeks 1 to 4: Diagnosing What Makes You Distinctive

In weeks 1 to 4, we base your positioning on booking evidence, not instinct.

  1. Collect booking history

    You send us at least 12 months of booking history, or 24 months if you have it. Export it from your PMS by date, room type, channel and rate code.

  2. Gather guest evidence

    We use whatever data you have on guest origins, lengths of stay, lead times and review scores, alongside competitive analysis and guest research.

  3. Label missing evidence

    We fill any gaps with comp-set rates, market demand data and your budget. We mark these figures as estimates and revisit the diagnostic as your own data replaces them.

  4. Identify distinctive strengths

    We compare your design, location, views and signature room types against your reviews and bookings to establish what guests value.

  5. Define guest segments

    From the evidence we've collected, we identify 3 to 4 target segments, such as weekend couples or design-minded travellers.

  6. Translate positioning into pricing

    We set base rates, rate floors and room-type differentials and price your distinctive rooms at a premium. We also match rate plans, minimum stays and packages to how each segment books.

What We Need From You Before Kickoff

Data access safeguards

Your data stays in your systems, and only our team has remote access to it. We keep our working files in access-controlled cloud storage that only our team can use. No subcontractor gets access to your account.

Our privacy policy sets out the details.

Access and ownership

Before kickoff, you give us your property details, user access to your systems and the name of the person responsible for the work. If any access is missing, the timeline stalls.

Calls and approvals

You join a 60-minute kickoff call, then remote working sessions every two weeks on Starter or every week on Core, and you reply to approval requests within 2 business days. Slow approvals delay delivery.

No marketing person

If you don't have a marketing person, we train your general manager or a staff member you nominate. We can also act as your interim marketing lead until your new hire is trained.

Weeks 5 to 10: Building Your Strategy and Pricing Framework

In weeks 5 to 10, we turn the diagnostic into an agreed foundation for the work that follows.

You'll see less marketing activity early on. That's because launching channels and advertising before your rates and positioning are settled creates rework. In this phase, we write your positioning and revenue strategy, set your pricing framework and agree both with you.

Through our Dynamic Pricing Strategy, we also tune your gap-night rules and booking policies to local events and demand, and bring your budget under the same revenue strategy.

  1. Build pricing framework

    Base rates and rate floors for each room type, seasonal, event and lead-time rules, minimum stays, length-of-stay pricing and channel markups, mapped on a 12-month forward calendar.

  2. Set channel priorities

    We decide which channels to prioritise and how pricing, availability and forecasts serve one revenue target.

  3. Rewrite your positioning

    Positioning is rewritten for your listings and website.

  4. Deliver written strategy

    A written strategy with quarterly milestones and a prioritised roadmap of immediate, short-term and medium-term actions.

  5. Agree in working session

    We review the strategy, pricing framework and roadmap with you and agree them together.

  6. Start distribution and marketing

    Channel and marketing work begins in week 11, built on settled rates and positioning.

Weeks 11 to 22: Direct Bookings, Paid Media and PR

Distribution and visibility

In weeks 11 to 22, we optimise your direct bookings, calibrate your OTA presence, run paid acquisition where it's justified and handle PR and lifestyle media.

Conversion before advertising

We only switch on paid channels once your booking engine converts at least 1.5% of visitors. Below that level, paid acquisition waits, and it doesn't start while conversion stays too low.

Cost-based channel retention

We run a 60-day test budget. We keep a channel only if its cost per direct booking stays below the OTA commission you'd have paid on that booking.

Paid channel selection

We use Google Hotel Ads, metasearch on Tripadvisor and Trivago, Google search ads on your brand and on high-intent searches, and Meta retargeting aimed at people who've already visited your website.

Your account ownership

Your team opens the ad and metasearch accounts in your property's name, pays the platforms directly with your own payment method and gives us user access. This way, the data and history stay with you.

PR materials and approvals

You give us a property fact sheet, 15 to 20 high-resolution photos and one contact who approves media requests within 2 business days.

Final Weeks: Handing the Work to Your Team

Every consulting engagement ends with remote training and handover.

In the final weeks, set aside time for your marketing, revenue, front office or sales staff to learn the routines over video call. Our documentation supports them as they learn.

We cover daily rate and restriction updates, weekly pace and pickup reviews, forecasting, OTA management, listing content and parity checks. Training also covers monthly occupancy, ADR and RevPAR reporting, plus your direct-booking and email marketing routines. After that, your team runs the work on its own.

Our documentation stays with your team so they can keep those routines running.

Fees, Billing and the Ongoing Advisory Retainer

  • Package fee drivers

    Your fee depends on how many OTA channels we manage, any website and booking-engine implementation, and how much paid acquisition and PR work is included.

  • Additional channel quotes

    If you want extra channels, including regional OTAs, we quote each one as a one-off setup fee per property after a short call about the scope.

  • Scope changes

    We price any work outside your agreed scope separately, and we only start it once you've agreed to it in writing.

  • Separate provider costs

    Budget separately for OTA commissions, software subscriptions and any advertising or metasearch spend. These aren't part of our fee. You pay them directly to the providers.

  • Optional ongoing advice

    Once your starter or core engagement ends, you can choose to take a monthly advisory retainer. It includes a monthly strategy check-in, quarterly planning and asynchronous advice by message or email.

  • Retainer decision support

    You get insights from comparable hotel clients. When a major decision comes up, you also get priority access to our team.

  • Billing and payment terms

    We bill monthly retainers upfront on the 1st, and payment is due within 14 days. You prepay one-time services when you book. Prices are in USD and don't include sales tax. You can pay by bank transfer or major credit card. Canadian clients also pay the applicable GST/HST. Our terms and conditions set out the full payment terms.

Measurable Targets and Proof From Similar Properties

We agree property-specific targets, then track your results against your own history and market, separating your performance from wider market movement.

We don't guarantee specific revenue, occupancy or ADR results. On request before you sign, we'll share case studies with before-and-after RevPAR, occupancy and ADR for hotels like yours in type and size, plus an anonymised sample monthly performance report.

How we set and track measurable targets, without guaranteeing specific revenue results.

  1. Build baseline in audit

    Your last 12 months of RevPAR, occupancy and ADR, and your position against a competitive set of 5 to 8 similar properties.

  2. Agree quarterly targets

    We agree measurable goals with you at the start for each quarterly milestone.

  3. Track in monthly reports

    Performance is measured against your own history and your market, so results can be checked.

  4. Written recovery plan

    If agreed targets are missed 2 months in a row, you receive a written recovery plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you work with a boutique hotel that is still pre-opening or being repositioned, and has no booking history yet?

Yes. We start up to 6 months before you open or relaunch, and we support your positioning, pricing, segmentation, distribution setup and early trading pace. If you don't have booking history or a mailing list yet, we work from comp-set rates, market demand and your budget. Your guest list grows from pre-opening sign-ups and early bookers. As your own booking data builds up, it replaces the estimates.

If I already have a marketing or PR agency, do you work alongside them or take over their scope?

We work alongside your agency and don't take over its scope. At the start, we agree in writing who owns each channel, task and decision. Under that agreement, we lead revenue strategy, OTA distribution and direct-booking conversion, and your agency keeps creative, PR and campaign execution. We share monthly reports and join a monthly coordination call.

Can you reshape our direct and OTA distribution while retaining existing OTA, wholesaler and room-allotment agreements?

Yes. We grow your direct bookings while using OTAs deliberately, and you don't drop any partners. Your existing contracts, allotments and negotiated corporate or wholesale rates stay in place unless you approve changes in writing.

How do you run guest research and the competitive analysis without visiting the property, and what data do you need from us?

We run the whole diagnostic remotely, with no site visits. We compare you with 5 to 8 properties matched on location, type, size and price. For this we use daily competitor rate checks, booking pace, pickup, and live market data from pricing tools and OTA extranets. For guest research, we use the OTA reviews and PMS profiles you have, plus a short email survey sent to guests after their stay. From you, we need your property details, access to your systems and at least 12 months of booking history, or 24 months if you have it.

Does either consulting package include daily rate, restriction and inventory decisions, or must we retain that responsibility or purchase managed revenue services separately?

No, neither consulting package includes those daily decisions. We build the strategy and pricing framework, and your team decides rates, restrictions and inventory. If you want us to make those daily decisions for every room type, we offer a separate monthly managed revenue management plan, which you can buy on its own or with consulting. You keep full control of your pricing, and we make major decisions together.

Can we remove or add parts of a package to fit what we need?

Yes. You can drop PR, lifestyle media or paid acquisition. Instead of the standard channel count, you can choose one OTA channel or every channel you sell on. You can take revenue management, OTA management and related online services separately or together. If you combine them, we deliver them as a bundle with priority turnaround.
