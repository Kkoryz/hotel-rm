Who Reads Your Message at RateHarbor

Every message you send is read by a person on our 12-person team, not by an automated system, and we reply within one business day. Our team covers revenue management, OTA management and owner reporting.

If you're already a client, your message goes straight to the dedicated specialist who owns your account.

Choosing the Best Way to Get in Touch

First enquiries and quotes

Send your enquiry or quote request through the form on this page or to hello@rateharbor.com, and include some basic details about your property.

That written context lets us route your message to the right person and prepare for our first conversation.

Quick questions by phone

Call us on +1 (416) 555-0187, Monday to Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time. If your question only needs a yes or no, calling gets you the answer faster than writing in.

Existing client discussions

Contact your dedicated specialist directly, or bring your question to your weekly or monthly strategy call. Your specialist already knows your account, so you don't have to explain it again.

What Happens After You Send a Message

We talk through your needs before quoting in writing, because your channel mix, systems and goals set the scope and price.

  1. Free consultation

    Once we've replied, we book a free 30-minute consultation to learn about your property, your goals and your current setup. You don't commit to anything.

  2. Written proposal and quote

    Within 5 business days of that call, we send you a written proposal with a free quote. There's no instant price, and even a rough figure waits for the proposal.

  3. Onboarding begins

    Once you've signed the proposal, whose fees then stay fixed, and given us access, we start onboarding with an audit of your property.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I reach someone outside business hours or at weekends?

You can send us a message outside office hours, and we answer evening and weekend enquiries within one business day. Our office hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Eastern Time. Once you're a client, your dedicated specialist and the rest of our team cover your account all 365 days of the year, so urgent rate or channel issues get handled at weekends too.

Can my general manager or accountant contact you for me?

Yes. Your general manager, accountant or management company can contact us for you, and we reply to them within one business day. Before we share any property figures or send a proposal, we confirm with you who can approve decisions and who receives reports. We can copy your named contacts on reports and strategy calls. Confirming these roles adds a step before work begins. It also keeps commercially sensitive rate and revenue data with the right people and prevents conflicting instructions.

Will what I write about my property be kept confidential?

Yes. What you tell us about your property stays confidential. Only our own team reads it, and no subcontractor ever has access. Before we receive any access or share any figures, we put confidentiality in writing through your NDA or our data processing agreement. We handle personal data under Canada's PIPEDA, and also under the GDPR and UK GDPR where your guests' data falls within them.

How soon will someone reply to my message?

We reply to every enquiry within one business day, including messages you send in the evening or at the weekend. The same response time applies when your general manager, accountant or management company contacts us for you.

From your first message to sharing property figures
