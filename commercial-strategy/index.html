Commercial Strategy and Supporting Services

You can buy revenue management, OTA management and our related online services on their own or together, and when we bundle several services, you get priority turnaround.

Our commercial strategy work brings planning and market analysis together with your leadership, and it includes KPI tracking and detailed performance reports. You can also add a revenue audit, a strategy roadmap, demand forecasting and budgeting, competitive set monitoring, OTA management and remote training for your team.

Strategic Planning, Market Analysis and Reporting

  • Net commercial performance

    We set your priorities around total revenue after channel costs, instead of judging performance on occupancy, ADR and RevPAR alone.

  • Revenue and channel reporting

    We track TRevPAR from packages, upsells and extras. We report channel revenue both gross and net of OTA commissions and acquisition costs, so you can see your net RevPAR. Your live KPI dashboard also shows the share of direct bookings.

  • Competitive market analysis

    Every day, we check rates at 5 to 8 properties matched to yours by location, type, size and price level, and we track booking pace, pickup and demand signals alongside them. Every quarter, we review and update your competitive set.

  • Your operating data

    We combine live market data from pricing tools and OTA extranets with your PMS, RMS and channel-manager history. We work in tools such as PriceLabs, Beyond Pricing, SiteMinder and Cloudbeds.

  • Written commercial plan

    Your fixed-scope roadmap includes a revenue audit, a written strategy and a 12-month calendar of monthly actions for your own team to carry out.

  • Priorities and departmental targets

    We set your pricing and distribution strategy, position your property against its competitive set, set KPI targets for each department and put your priorities in order.

  • Remote working model

    Our working process is fully remote. We work inside your hotel’s systems and online accounts, and we have no staff on site at any property.

  • Evidence before signing

    If you ask before you sign, we'll share full case studies for properties of a similar type and size, showing RevPAR, occupancy and ADR before and after. We'll also send an anonymised sample monthly performance report.

Related Revenue and Distribution Services

  • Revenue management

    Our revenue management service covers pricing and rate strategy, demand forecasting, competitive set monitoring and regular performance reports for owners.

  • Hotel distribution

    Our Hotel Distribution service allocates your inventory across OTAs, GDS and direct channels based on what each one returns after commission. It also controls pricing and availability to keep rate parity and reduce the risk of overbooking.

  • OTA management

    Our OTA management service runs your listings on Booking.com, Expedia and other OTAs, covering content, rates, rate parity and channel-manager settings.

  • Revenue management audit

    After a free 30-minute consultation, we quote a fixed fee for single-property revenue audits. We deliver within 10 business days of receiving your data and access, and we add 10 business days for each additional batch of up to 5 properties.

  • Boutique hotel consulting

    Our Boutique Hotel Consulting runs as a 3-month starter or a 6-month core engagement, and the core engagement includes an audit, competitive-set analysis, a written strategy, quarterly milestones, active management of 4 to 6 OTA channels, weekly remote sessions, SOPs and training.

Group Sales and Revenue Team Collaboration

  • Contracted business strategy

    We set the pricing and availability strategy for your corporate, tour operator and wholesale business, not just your public transient rates.

  • Tailored group quotes

    We prepare tailored quotes for business and leisure groups, including private celebrations, events and trade fairs.

  • Displacement analysis

    For every group enquiry, we compare total group revenue with the transient demand forecast for those dates. This shows whether the group would displace more valuable bookings.

  • Minimum acceptable rates

    Your sales team gets minimum acceptable rates by date and room type, refreshed every week as booking pace changes. Your team still handles negotiation and closing.

  • Existing agreement protection

    Your contracts, allotments and negotiated corporate or wholesale rates stay as they are unless you approve changes in writing.

  • Sales initiative tracking

    We measure the results of each sales initiative in weekly and monthly reports. You also get an in-depth review every quarter and live KPI dashboards between reports.

  • Joint performance reviews

    We go through those sales results with you on your weekly or monthly strategy call.

  • Leadership financial reporting

    Your senior leadership gets regular financial reports and forecasts showing how each initiative affects revenue against budget.

Commercial Technology Selection and Setup

Connected system selection

We look at sales CRM, group and event management, upselling and ancillary-sales platforms alongside PMS, RMS, CRS, channel managers and pricing tools. We choose the systems together so they fit your commercial plan.

Vendor comparison criteria

For each system, we compare 3 to 4 vendors against your written requirements, their integrations with your existing PMS and channel manager, their total annual cost and live demos that you attend.

Independent recommendations

We have no reseller or referral partnerships. We take no referral commissions from software vendors or OTAs, and we don't claim official partner status with Booking.com, Expedia or any other platform.

Your existing systems

We don't sell software and we aren't tied to any RMS, so you don't need any particular system. You keep your current RMS, PMS, CRS and channel manager, and we help you use them better and recommend configuration changes.

Installation and staff training

Once you've chosen a system, we install and configure it, then train your staff to use it. Selection and setup carry their own fixed fee, quoted after the assessment.

Commercial Strategy: Engagement Options and Pricing

  • Scope matched to capacity

    If you have capable revenue staff in-house, you choose a fixed-scope project. If you don't have that capacity, you choose an ongoing monthly programme with a one-time setup and onboarding.

  • Property complexity and scale

    Your fee reflects how many properties you have, how complex they are and the scope of the work, which runs from daily pricing to forecasting and full commercial strategy. Select-service hotels need less work than full-service independents.

  • Technology and provider costs

    Technology selection and setup carry a fixed fee, which we quote after the assessment. You pay software subscriptions and OTA commissions directly to the providers.

  • Fixed project terms

    Audits and strategy roadmaps come with a fixed scope and a fixed fee. A standalone audit doesn't require any continuing engagement, and every consulting engagement ends with remote staff training and handover.

  • Flat monthly retainer

    Your monthly retainer is a flat fee. There's no commission, no percentage of room revenue and no performance-linked bonus.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does commercial strategy differ from your outsourced revenue management or revenue consulting in what you do, what our team does and what results we should expect?

With commercial strategy, we set the direction with your leadership. With outsourced revenue management, we make the daily revenue decisions. With fixed-scope consulting, we give you an action plan for your existing revenue team to implement. Commercial strategy covers revenue management, marketing, sales and distribution, and it targets total revenue and channel profitability over a 12-month plan. With fully outsourced revenue management, your dedicated specialist decides daily rates, restrictions and inventory for every room type on a monthly retainer, and the target is day-to-day gains in occupancy, ADR and RevPAR. Fixed-scope consulting delivers an audit, a written report and a strategy roadmap. The right choice depends on whether someone in your organisation has the time and authority to act on pricing and commercial decisions. With commercial strategy, your team carries out the plan day to day, while we load the offers we design; system setup carries its own fixed fee. To get started, you share access and data and we plan with your leadership. After that come ongoing strategy calls and approvals.

Can you build a commercial strategy for a newly opened or repositioned property without representative trading history?

Yes. If you don't have representative trading history, we build your commercial strategy from competitive-set rates, market demand data and your budget. Then we switch to your own data as bookings build. We pick 5 to 8 comparable properties that match your new location, type, size and price level. During the first 12 months, we revisit the strategy with you every 6 to 8 weeks, until your own history can lead decisions.

Does your ancillary-revenue work include setting prices and designing packages or upsell offers, or only identifying opportunities for our team?

It includes setting prices, designing packages and upsell offers, and loading them into your systems. We don't stop at identifying opportunities. We price add-ons such as parking, breakfast and early check-in, design room packages and pre-arrival upsell offers, and load them as rate plans in your booking engine, PMS and OTA extranets. Your team approves each offer and delivers the service at the property. We don't run hotel operations. Each month, we report take-up and revenue for every offer.

How do you determine whether our commercial goals require a new system rather than better use of tools we already license?

We recommend a new system only in two cases: your commercial goal can't be met by reconfiguring your existing tools or making full use of them, or the workaround would cost your team more than 5 hours a week. First, we audit how your PMS, channel manager, RMS and CRM are configured and used. Then we map each goal to features you already license. We don't accept referral commissions from software vendors or OTAs, so we base our recommendation only on what fits your property.

What your leadership commits to for joint strategic planning

  1. Share access and data

    User access to your systems, at least 12 months of booking history (24 if available), plus your current budget and P&L statements for the same period.

  2. Kickoff call

    A 60-minute kickoff call with your leadership.

  3. Two planning sessions

    Two 90-minute planning sessions in the first 4 weeks, with the person who can approve decisions present.

  4. Ongoing strategy calls

    A weekly or monthly strategy call after that, with replies to approval requests within 2 business days.
